ITEM TO CREATE: Mage's Staff

Style requirements:

The object should be isolated, centered, and fully visible, with a clean transparent background. Use dark brown or black hand-drawn outlines, soft painterly shading, muted earthy medieval colors, and subtle highlights. The item should look practical, worn, and usable rather than ornate or overtly magical.

A Mage's personal spell-focus staff that also functions as a sturdy two-handed quarterstaff in combat and a walking staff on long journeys. A single long straight length of tough weathered brown hardwood, roughly human height, with believable weight and thickness suitable for striking and parrying.
