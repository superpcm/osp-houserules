# Listening and racial detection

| Race | Hearing | Secret doors | Construction tricks | Room traps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human | 1-in-6 | 1-in-6 | — | 1-in-6 general search |
| Dwarf | 2-in-6 | 1-in-6 | 2-in-6 | 2-in-6 stonework |
| Elf | 2-in-6 | 2-in-6 | — | 1-in-6 general search |
| Gnome | 2-in-6 | 1-in-6 | 2-in-6 | 1-in-6 general search |
| Half-Elf | 1-in-6 | 2-in-6 | — | 1-in-6 general search |
| Half-Orc | 1-in-6 | 1-in-6 | — | 1-in-6 general search |
| Hobbit | 2-in-6 | 1-in-6 | — | 1-in-6 general search |

For Thief and Assassin, hearing is the better of the racial chance and trained Hear Noise, never their sum. Training begins at 1-in-6 and caps at 5-in-6. Racial hearing does not change the stored training value or spend/grant Skill Points. Approved by the user, 2026-10-02.

Use the actor’s **Exploration** control for GM-private checks. Roll equal to or below the chance on a d6. The referee confirms time, location, circumstances and retry eligibility and reveals only what the character perceives. Success/failure and the existence of hidden objects are not sent to players.

Construction and room-trap detection concern the relevant racial stonework abilities. Thief Find/Remove Traps concerns small mechanisms and remains a separate trained skill. See [BR-17 support contract](BR-17-EXPLORATION-AND-SKILLS.md).
