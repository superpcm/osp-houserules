# OSP House Rules

A Foundry VTT v14 game system for the Old School Player group's house rules. Players use character sheets, party tools, Bank, Storehouse, and now GM-controlled equipment merchants during play.

Stores serve two jobs: the GM prepares reusable merchant catalogs and controls party access; players select equipment, review carrying locations and cost, and purchase using physically carried coins. Spending must preserve character inventory, stock, and receipts through interruptions and retries.

The store is an extension of the established parchment character-sheet and Bank world. The user selected Parchment Ledger (concept 1), explicitly rejecting Merchant Shelves. Preserve the approved walnut header, brass frame, ruled parchment inventory rows, burgundy controls, and fixed cart. Do not substitute a shelf/grid layout.

The whole party can shop only when the GM opens a store. Players purchase through Stores in the Player's Toolkit. GM grants, owned transfers, and existing Bank/Storehouse withdrawal locking retain their own workflows.

First-version limits: physical equipment only, carried coin payment, manual stock editing. No selling, refunds, automated restock, bank/treasury payments, creatures, or vehicles. Prices use the system's silver scale: 1 gp = 10 sp = 100 cp; arithmetic uses integer copper.
